Every album you choose, every letter you write, teaches me something new about who you are and how you move through life.

I love hearing what you hear.

This project has given us a way to talk without rushing, to listen without interrupting, and to share pieces of ourselves that might not come up otherwise. It’s helped me understand you more deeply, and it’s reminded me how much I admire the person you’ve become.

Most of all, it’s reminded me how much I love you — not just as my daughter, but as you.
