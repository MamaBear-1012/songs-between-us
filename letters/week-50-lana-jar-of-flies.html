Week 50

From: Lana

Artist: Alice in Chains

Album: Jar of Flies

Kayla,

This week I have Jar of Flies by Alice in Chains. This came out the year Chloe was born.

It is intense, dark, and brooding, covering some of the most difficult parts of being human: loss, emptiness, and loneliness. Yet somehow, it is all packaged in really beautiful music.

It is a tough listen, especially when you tie in Layne Staley's struggles with addiction and his eventual death from an overdose.

Everyone around him, his mother, friends, and bandmates, tried to help him, but they just couldn't change the outcome. I feel a special kinship with his mother, and I appreciate her candidness about addiction and loss.

I fell in love with the song "I Stay Away," which is so beautiful. You will remember this one. When I think of this song, I think of us in Ridgefield, driving the back way to Toys R Us by that farm with the cows, and this song playing.

"Don't Follow" was what I listened to after Laura died, trying to make sense of how her life unfolded. In a million years, I would have never thought this would be a song that I think of with Chloe. It makes me cry every time I hear it.

I like all of the songs. I love Layne Staley's voice (even when I can't understand what he is saying), and the music is so beautiful.

I hope Chloe is friends with him in heaven.

I hope you like this one.

I love you.

Mom
