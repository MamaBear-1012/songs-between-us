Week 44

From: Kayla

Artist: Basement

Album: I Wish I Could Stay Here

Mom,

My pick for you this week is I Wish I Could Stay Here by Basement. I think my undying love for this band and their sound has been explained a lot by your recent albums. I think they capture the grunge sound so perfectly.

These guys are from the UK. So they don’t come here much, and I’ve never gotten to see them. I think I’ve had tickets twice, and one time they played before I got there because I was late, and then the other time they opened for The Front Bottoms, but it was around all the ISIS shit so I got scared and didn’t go. So they are on my bucket list to see.

Ok, but anyway, this album is so freaking good. I love every song. I will say “Crickets Throw Their Voice” is one of my favorite songs in the world. It’s always one of my top played songs every year on Spotify since like 2012 haha.

I think you’ll like this. It’s got some really great punk and grunge influence.

Kayla
