From: Kayla

Artist: Bikini Kill

Album: The First Two Records

Mom,

My pick for you this week is Bikini Kill.

These guys are legends. They are a part of the riot grrrl movement, which was a ’90s punk feminist wave that came out of Seattle. Kathleen Hanna is the lead singer. She used to hang out with Kurt Cobain, and she spray-painted “Kurt Smells Like Teen Spirit” on his wall, which became the song title for Nirvana’s huge hit. Courtney Love makes fun of them in her song “Rock Star” on Live Through This.

I got into Bikini Kill in high school. Molly introduced me to them when she noticed I only listened to girl-fronted bands. I love how funny the songs are while still saying political stuff.

My favorites:

  • Double Dare Ya
  • Liar
  • Carnival
  • Suck My Left One
  • Feels Blind
  • White Boy
  • This Is Not a Test
  • Resist Psychic Death
  • Rebel Girl
  • Girl Soldier
