Kayla,

For this week. Another one I was torn on. So the winner is Peace Thru Vandalism by The Vandals.

The Vandals are one of my favorite punk bands. This album - the original one had more songs - but I think that they have had legal issues with band members so this is what it is reduced to. It’s short but still worthy of my list.

Another album that reminds me of my friendship with Lisa. We played this one a lot driving around.

What I love is the humor. When punk first emerged in my world it was either funny or politically motivated. Some bands can do both - but I always found the Vandals funny.

I like all the songs. But my #1 favorite is Mohawk Town, not on this album but I will send separately, which depicts the difference of the punk world between skinheads and Mohawks.

Next is Urban Struggle which might be the funniest song about going to a cowboy bar.

Next is The Legend of Pat Brown. It is pretty violent - but I love it anyway.

Wanna Be Manor: I do like this one too but recently realized what the song is about. So I can’t say I like it as much as I used to.

I hope you have fun listening to this one.

I love you!!

Mom
