Week 17 · Lana

Peace Thru Vandalism

The Vandals

For this week — another one I was torn on. So the winner is Peace Thru Vandalism by The Vandals.

The Vandals are one of my favorite punk bands. This album — the original one had more songs — but I think they’ve had legal issues with band members, so this is what it’s been reduced to. It’s short, but still worthy of my list.

Another album that reminds me of my friendship with Lisa. We played this one a lot driving around.

What I love is the humor. When punk first emerged in my world it was either funny or politically motivated. Some bands can do both — but I always found The Vandals funny.

Favorites & standouts

I like all the songs. But my number one favorite is Mohawk Town — not on this album, but I’ll send it separately — which depicts the difference in the punk world between skinheads and Mohawks.

Next is Urban Struggle, which might be the funniest song about going to a cowboy bar. Then The Legend of Pat Brown. It’s pretty violent — but I love it anyway.

Wanna Be Manor

I do like this one too, but I recently realized what the song is about — so I can’t say I like it as much as I used to.

I hope you have fun listening to this one.
