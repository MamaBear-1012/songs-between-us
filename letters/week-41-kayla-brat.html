Artist: Charli XCX

Album: Brat

Hi Julia!

This week I am sharing Brat by Charli XCX.

There is so much to say about this album. First, I hated this album when I first heard it. Charli is a house DJ from the UK and I hate EDM. I am very close-minded and think it sounds like someone turning a computer on and off. I don’t see any musical merit in that. So I wrote this album off despite rave critic reviews and everyone I know being like, it’s amazing.

That was until I saw her perform at Coachella and then I started to get it. And slowly, song by song, I fell in love with Brat. Where now I would say it’s easily one of the best pop albums of all time. It’s better than anything Britney Spears or Spice Girls or anybody has put out. It’s an incredible time capsule of our current culture.

The lyrics are insanely simple and almost dumb. And at first, I hated it, but then I realized I think it’s part of this larger artistic statement she’s making. The album is very raw and honest. I think she describes what being a girl is like so well. The highs and the lows.

She reminds me a little of Sleigh Bells. This might be a tough listen for you and I’m sorry ahead of time. I’m hoping you can start to hear what I hear. But at first you are probably going to think, wtf is this.

I love all the songs. The marketing on this album was brilliant. The album cover is so genius. And they did a million amazing things with it. Like everything about this album was historic.

“Girl, So Confusing” was a song Charli wrote about her friendship with Lorde and feeling so uncertain in it. She sent the demo to Lorde and Lorde wrote a verse kinda responding to it. It’s just a great song about women and friendship and pressure that gets put on us. And especially in their industry. It’s just remarkably honest from both of them. They performed at Coachella and it was incredible.
