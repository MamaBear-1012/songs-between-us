Week 35

From: Kayla

Artist: Lil Peep

Album: Crybaby

Mom,

My pick for you this week is Crybaby by Lil Peep. I’m afraid this might not be one of your favorite weeks, but I couldn’t skip it.

Peep was my #1 most played artist from 2017–2022. I picked this album for a couple of reasons.

One being that he is from SoundCloud. In our music journey, we started with your vinyls and cassette tapes, and Peep comes from the latest form of music and distribution: an online streaming platform for music. Billie Eilish also started on SoundCloud. A lot of these albums you couldn’t even get on Spotify or Apple Music until 2023 and later. It’s kind of like the online version of underground music.

Two, Peep is a pioneer of the genre emo rap. Before his death, he was expected to become huge. Emo and rap? My two fave genres of music!!

He died in November 2017 from an accidental overdose.

And three, Peep helped me tremendously with my grief around Kayla Hay and her addiction to drugs. He had a girlfriend from high school that he was on and off with, and a lot of his songs are about his struggle with caring about her but being so addicted to drugs that he can’t show up. It just helped me start untangling myself from it.

Also, I have a very silly belief that him and Chloe are married in heaven.

I love this album, but I think this might be like when Britney Spears did her version of “(I Can’t Get No) Satisfaction” for you 😂😂. But we’ll see haha.

There are at least three songs that are sampled on this album that I think you should be able to recognize.

I love them all, but my absolute favorites are:

  • Crybaby
  • Lil Jeep
  • Absolute in Doubt
  • Ghost Girl
  • Nineteen
  • White Tee
  • Driveway

Kayla
