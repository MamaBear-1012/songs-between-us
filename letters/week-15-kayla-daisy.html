Week 15 – Kayla

My pick for you this week is Daisy by Brand New.

This was another hard decision when it came to deciding which album to bring you in on. Brand New came before and influenced Manchester Orchestra and The Front Bottoms, Modern Baseball, Basement. They are second wave of emo and they take emo and rock/punk/alt and mix it together.

Tamar put me on to them when we first moved in together. And later that summer this album came out and we drove around and listened to the whole thing on repeat.

I love the whole tone and atmosphere of this album. It’s a very southern gothic vibe to it.

My favorite songs: Vices, You Stole, Be Gone, Sink, Daisy, Noro.

I love Bed and At the Bottom — those used to be my first favorites when I listened.

For some reason Apple doesn’t give the album without the two live versions of the song at the end. Weiner.
