This week I am going with Love by The Cult.

It has been on my list for a few weeks and I keep pushing it back.

I played this on cassette in my car more times than I can count, well into the hundreds. The album as a whole makes me think about listening to this and driving around.

“She Sells Sanctuary” was really popular in clubs, and that song in particular reminds me of Lisa and our many nights at Graffiti’s.

I love the lead singer’s voice. He can be hard to understand, but his voice is unique.

Standouts for me:
Love, Brother Wolf Sister Moon, Phoenix, Revolution (but honestly they are all good).

The album is considered hard rock, but at the time it was really more alternative, because at the time hard rock was hair bands.

The next album they did was super hard rock, and everyone I hung out with thought they had been kidnapped and brainwashed to go from this amazing music to that.

I hope you like this one.

Love you🥰
