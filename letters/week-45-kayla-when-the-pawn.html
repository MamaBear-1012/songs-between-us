Week 45

From: Kayla

Artist: Fiona Apple

Album: When the Pawn…

Mom,

My pick for you this week is When the Pawn… by Fiona Apple.

Interesting fact: The album title is much longer and is the second longest title ever in music history. The full title is a 400-word-long poem.

I really was stuck between which album to send of hers. I had another album of hers I listened to in high school that I loved, but I think this is her best work and overall the one I connect the most with.

Some of Fiona’s songs always start really slow and I get impatient, so a lot of this album grew on me as I matured and sat through it, cause really the way they start never really is how the rest of the song goes. I’ll say whenever I hear this album I think whoever hurt her must be very embarrassed because this album is pretty harsh haha. Reminds me of Jagged Little Pill in that way.

I think as I started to have more self-esteem and see things a little more clearly with certain people, this album was really cathartic. And also getting so sick of my own blind spots. Some of her lyrics are so clever and raw.

Favorites:

  • On the Bound
  • Limp
  • Love Ridden
  • Paper Bag
  • A Mistake
  • Fast as You Can
  • Get Gone
  • I Know

P.S.: There’s a fun little music breakdown in “Limp” and I like to dance crazy whenever that comes on.
