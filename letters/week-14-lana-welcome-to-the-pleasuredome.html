Week 14 – Lana

This week I was torn. So I wrote my two picks on little pieces of paper and chose blindly.

The winner: Frankie Goes to Hollywood – Welcome to the Pleasuredome.

Absolutely epic album.

The song Relax was very popular on MTV, on the radio, and in clubs.

But the rest of the album is just sooo good.

There are some x-rated ones that I don’t like (Tag, Ballad of 32). I don’t think I understood that Relax was sexual - which is kind of silly when I think about it now.

I love the opening song: The World Is My Oyster. I love the closing song: Bang.

The second song: Welcome to the Pleasuredome just sets the tone for the entire album.

Standouts for me: War, Wish the Lads Were Here, Fury, Two Tribes, The Only Star in Heaven, Black Night White Light.

Power of Love is probably my top favorite - that was the song Anthony and I danced to when we got married.

The remake of Bruce Springsteen’s Born to Run is so nice. Funny story I thought this was a Frankie song. The first time I heard Bruce’s version I thought he was doing the Frankie song. I don’t remember who I was with when I found this out. But I strongly thought they were idiots to not know that this was a Frankie original. 🫢

It is just a treat and I hope you love it. ❤️❤️
