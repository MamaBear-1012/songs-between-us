Week 4 — Lana

Van Halen — Van Halen

Are you ready for Week 4?

I almost forgot about this one. I didn’t own it — Laura did. And I would secretly borrow it and listen to this album. It was a game changer in hard rock. It came out when I was 12, in junior high.

This was when I was really heavy into gymnastics, and when Laura started partying really hard.

This is Van Halen’s first album, and it was remarkable at the time. David Lee Roth, the lead singer, had such an interesting voice — with cool screams. He was drop-dead gorgeous, had the most beautiful smile, and was an incredible showman.

Then there’s Eddie Van Halen. He played lead guitar like no one else on the planet. I’m sure he’s still known as one of the greatest guitar players of all time.

And Michael Anthony — he’s what gives Van Halen their harmonies.
