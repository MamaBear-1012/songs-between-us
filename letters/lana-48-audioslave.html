Kayla,

This week Audioslave self titled album.

Saw the music video for “Cochise.” I was like who the fuck is that? The lead singer was like amazing - the absolute definition of a rock star. It was incredible.

Turns out it’s Chris Cornell with short hair from Soundgarden. His eyes are so beautiful. He formed the band with the guitarist, bassist and drummer from Rage Against the Machine. Tom Morello is a genius.

The music and his voice is incredible.

The first half is so freaking good I think it kinda falls off towards the end - but I am not sure if it is because the first half is just mind blowingly great.

Lots of airplay for: “Like A Stone” and “I Am The Highway.”

My #1 favorite is: “Set It Off.” So fun to sing to.

Next: “Cochise” for the video and “Show Me How to Live” for the lyrics.

Also good: “What You Are” a great over your bullshit song. “Shadow of the Sun” makes me think of Chloe and Laura.

Know this all the sounds you hear are the instruments: guitar, bass, drums and Chris Cornell’s voice and the bassist backing vocals.

Obviously play it loud 🤘

I am pretty sure you will start singing like Chris Cornell 🤣

I think you will love this one.

Love you!!!
