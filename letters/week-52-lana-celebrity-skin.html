Week 52

From: Lana

Artist: Hole

Album: Celebrity Skin

Kayla,

Today marks one year of sharing our music.

I have gotten to know you and see you from a unique perspective. Not as your mom, or you as my daughter, but you as an individual. It has been an incredible experience.

We agreed that we would start Year 2 with Hole.

I am leading with Celebrity Skin.

What I love about this album is that it is a mature look at being a woman: falling in love, surviving, and getting back on your feet. I wouldn't be so fast to give Billy Corgan so much credit. I think Courtney was able to get to the darkest of places and be vulnerable.

I literally love every song on this album. So fun to sing to.

"Dying": I loved where she went with this one. It's so raw, but today I relate to it on a different level. Same with "Use Once & Destroy."

"Boys on the Radio," "Hit So Hard," "Awful," "Reasons to Be Beautiful," and "Heaven Tonight" are so, so good.

"Petals" makes me think of Laura so much.

My take on Live Through This.

This is a bold and brave album. Courtney Love was not well liked and was portrayed horribly in the media because of addiction. I'm not sure anyone took her or her band seriously. But this album proved that she deserved respect, and she kicked the fucking door down.

This is girl power and an expression of what it is like to be female in your younger years. I love the punk feel to this one.

"Violet" just sets the whole album up. It stops you dead in your tracks and makes you pay attention. It is brilliant.

I love all the songs on this album, but my top favorites and standouts are "Violet" (duh), "Miss World," "Asking for It," "Doll Parts," "Softer, Softest," "She Walks on Me," "I Think That I Would Die," and "Rock Star."

That we both love this band makes me so happy, and honestly, I think about you more than anyone else when I listen to them.

A bucket list item for me is to see them play live with you.

I love you the mostest!!!! 🥰🥰🥰

Mom
