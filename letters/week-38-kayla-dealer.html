Week 38

From: Kayla

Artist: Foxing

Album: Dealer

Mom,

My pick for you this week is Dealer by Foxing. This is their sophomore album.

When this came out, I was driving from Greenwich to Westport for the pizza delivery job on the weekends. On Fridays, the traffic was so bad that I would have to leave at 12 pm so I wouldn’t be stuck in horrible traffic. But work wouldn’t start until 5 pm, so I would go to Westport Library and study or work on my book, and I listened to this album all the time.

When I was at UConn, I would walk to the public library in the city there and study for hours in between classes and listen to this album.

This album is so beautiful. I love his voice, and I love how cinematic the music feels. I am pretty sure this is the album they locked themselves in a remote cabin in Vermont over the winter and wrote this entire thing. So cool. I would love to do that.

I really love every song, but my absolute favorites are:

  • Weave
  • The Magdalene
  • Night Channels
  • Laundered
  • Indica
  • Glass Coughs
  • Redwoods

“Three on a Match” makes me think of Chloe and think of all three of us. It’s so beautiful.

I hope you like it ❤️

Kayla
