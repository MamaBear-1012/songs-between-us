Artist: A Flock of Seagulls

Album: Listen

Kayla,

This week I chose Listen by A Flock of Seagulls.

Welcome to the 80’s. I am not sure I would say out loud to a lot of people that I like this band. They really get a bad rap, but honestly I think it’s unfair.

I loved this album. It is super “synthy,” but behind that are just some really cool songs and beautiful lyrics.

“Wishing” was super popular and got a lot of airplay on regular radio.

They also did really well on MTV because of their funky hair-dos.

I saw them live at the Arizona State Fair, and it was one of the funnest concerts.

My favorites are:

  • Nightmares
  • Transfer Affection
  • The Fall

They definitely have an outer space vibe about them.

But it’s fun and catchy. I hope you like this one. 🥰

Mom
