Week 31

From: Kayla

Artist: Sorority Noise

Album: Joy, Departed

Mom,

My pick for you this week is Joy, Departed by Sorority Noise.

These guys are from Hartford, Connecticut! I listened to them a lot in my mid to late twenties. They’re in the same genre as Front Bottoms and Modern Baseball.

I think Brandon, the lead singer, writes such great songs that capture what being a struggling twenty-year-old is like.

I saw these guys in a really small venue somewhere in Connecticut with another one of my favorites I’ve yet to send you. We were waiting in line on these stairs to go up to the venue and he walked right by us. It was an amazing show.

There’s a guest singer in the song “Corrigan.” Can you tell who it is?

Listening back, I really love every song off this album. This was another pizza delivery album.

Big standouts for me are:

  • Corrigan
  • Nolsey
  • Art School Wannabe
  • Using
  • mononokay
  • when i see you

I think you will like them. He’s very honest like TFB, but a little more alternative.

Kayla
