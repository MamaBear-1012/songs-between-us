Week 12 — Lana

Album: All the Rage

Week 12.

This is a tough one. I could easily go on with The Cure. But I gotta go back — so here is General Public.

My friend Karen from high school was really into ska music, and she was, in my eyes, way cooler than most every other person I had met up to this point. We became friends my senior year and stayed friends until we moved from AZ. This is how I came to know General Public.

They are a spin-off from the band English Beat. It is just fun music. I know all the words.

I saw them in concert in Phoenix at an old movie theater that had a stage. It was one of the funnest concerts I have ever been to. When you describe The Front Bottoms concerts with the fans singing along — this is what I think of.

I like all of the songs. My least favorite is Hot You’re Cool — but that is probably because it has become so commercial.

I hope you dance around to this one and sing along. It is feel-good, happy music.

I love you.

— Lana
