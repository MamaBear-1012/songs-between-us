Week 8 — Kayla

Lifted or The Story Is in the Soil, Keep Your Ear to the Ground — Bright Eyes

Here’s my album for this week: Lifted or the Story Is in the Soil, Keep Your Ear to the Ground by Bright Eyes.

Conor Oberst is my favorite songwriter. This album has some of my absolute favorite examples of his songwriting. There are several songs on here that I would put in my personal top 10.

I don’t know why, but his voice reminds me of Robert Smith sometimes. And I believe he was heavily influenced by The Cure, so I’ve always thought of you when I listen to him — like, how cool that your band helped shape my favorite songwriter.

I’ve been listening to this album since I was 21, so it’s been through everything with me.

The song You Will. You? Will. You? Will. — me and Tamar always used to do a funny bit where we’d go “you will? will you? you will?” so I always laugh to myself when I see it come on.

Make War I listened to when I was taking a shuttle back from Kayla Wray’s to the airport after we broke up. It was like 6am and the sun was coming up over the desert.

A Day Is Gonna Come, Waste of Paint, and Let’s Not Shit Ourselves are some of my favorite songs ever in the entire world. Bowls of Oranges is such a great song too.

I think he has a really beautiful view on life and being a human — even with all the sadness.

— Kayla
