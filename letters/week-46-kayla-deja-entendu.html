Week 46

From: Kayla

Artist: Brand New

Album: Deja Entendu

Mom,

My pick for you this week is Deja Entendu by Brand New. This is their second album which followed your favorite one. This is probably considered their best album amongst fans. Everyone has a tattoo of the astronaut.

This album reminds me so much of being in my early 20’s and my friendship with Tamar. We used to listen to this album and sing our heads off, especially the song, “Okay I Believe You,” even when I listen to it now I can hear Tamar singing along.

This album has some of my favorite Brand New songs in their discography. The ones I really love are:

  • Tautou
  • Sic Transit
  • I Will Play My Game (have a tattoo for this one)
  • Okay I Believe You
  • Quiet Things
  • Boy Who Blocked His Own Shot
  • Jaws Theme
  • Me vs. Maradona
  • Play Crack the Sky

Sic Transit and Quiet Things, I wasn’t really a big fan of those songs until I saw Brand New play them live. I think the first 6 songs are just so freaking good. I really hear a lot of The Cure influence in some of their sound in this.

I hope you like this one. It’s a classic. I love you!

Kayla
