Week 10 — Lana

Album: Generation X

For Week 10.

One of my all-time favorites — Generation X.

Billy Idol was really popular in pop music, and I did like him a little bit in high school.

I was dating a guy named Travis, and he played this for me. I remember distinctly that we were in his apartment in Phoenix and he put this on — he played just Kiss Me Deadly because he thought it was the best song. I was like, Billy Idol?? Hmm… I don’t think so — and then it started and seriously, it was another big shift in my musical journey.

I would tell someone this is really a million times cooler than what Billy Idol eventually became.

I think of Lisa the most when I listen to this album. We played this all the time in her Mazda RX-7. We sang at the top of our lungs. I would say Kiss Me Deadly is a punk anthem.

I am always surprised Marky Ramone doesn’t play this on his station. He plays the mainstays all the time — One Hundred Punks, Ready Steady Go, Day By Day, Youth Youth Youth, Your Generation. Other ones that I think are overlooked: From the Heart, Kleenex, and Promises, Promises.

I love this one so much!!

— Lana
