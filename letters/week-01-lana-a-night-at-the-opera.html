A Night at the Opera — Queen

This is one of the first records I owned. I played it over and over and over when I was in junior high. It is really fun to sing to and has very different musical styles.

And lastly, Bohemian Rhapsody.

I remember the first time hearing that song on the radio — late at night with Laura in our trundle beds. And the trending debate about how long the song was, over 6 minutes.
