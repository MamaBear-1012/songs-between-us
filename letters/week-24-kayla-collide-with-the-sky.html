From: Kayla

Artist: Pierce the Veil

Album: Collide With the Sky

Mom,

My pick for you this week is Collide With the Sky by Pierce the Veil.

This album is probably in my top ten and one of the albums I would bring on an island if I had to be stranded and could only bring a few.

Pierce the Veil was big in the early 2010s, and they are huge among millennials and Gen Z. The guy’s voice is so distinct.

This album is so much fun to rock out to. I really hope you enjoy it.

My absolute favorites:

  • Hell Above
  • One Hundred Sleepless Nights
  • Match into Water
  • Bulls in the Bronx
  • King for a Day
  • Props & Mayhem
  • Hold On Till May
