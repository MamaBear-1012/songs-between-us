      <h1>Week 2 — Kayla</h1>
      <h2>A Black Mile To The Surface — Manchester Orchestra</h2>

      <p>
        This is my pick for you this week. This album came out the summer after Chloe died,
        so I always think of her when I listen to it.
      </p>

      <p>
        I listened to it over and over on my two-hour drive to UConn Waterbury for my
        Spanish summer class, taking Route 8 through all those gorgeous valleys in
        Connecticut.
      </p>

      <p>
        I love so much about this album. My favorite thing is how every song leads
        into the next — it feels like one long emotional thread.
      </p>

      <p>
        I get tears in my eyes at the first song. <em>The Alien</em> makes me think of Chloe
        the most. There’s a line he sings the whole time that repeats something like
        “didn’t mean to,” and I always think of it as her message to me — like she really
        didn’t mean to die.
      </p>

      <p>
        That song flows right into <em>The Sunshine</em>, which he wrote for his daughter,
        and I love that song so much too. The final song, <em>The Silence</em>, is my favorite.
        The last verse is for his daughter and it just gets me every time.
      </p>

      <p>
        The lead singer is Andy Hull, and his dad was a pastor, so there’s a lot of struggle
        with God in these songs — trying to find yourself, trying to understand pain.
        It feels very hymn-like in a way that’s hard to explain.
      </p>

      <p class="sbw-signature">— Kayla</p>
    </div>
