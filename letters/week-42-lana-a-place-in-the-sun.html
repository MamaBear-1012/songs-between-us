Kayla,

This week from London I am sending you A Place in the Sun by Lit.

I think you will recognize the music and the album cover. I love this album and listened to it hundreds and hundreds of times.

I listened to this a lot driving back and forth from CT and Mass.

It is a fun listen to sing along with and rock out to.

My faves:

  • Four — has the longest, coolest, and most unique intro ever. You might think they are two songs.
  • My Own Worst Enemy — was, I think, their most popular. They played this song at the Red Wings game and everyone knew the words.
  • Miserable — also got a lot of airplay.
  • Down
  • No Big Thing
  • Perfect One — is beautiful.

Fun sing alongs:

  • Quicksand
  • Zip-Lock
  • Lovely Day
  • A Place in the Sun

I nearly named every song and I feel like an ass for not calling them all out…. This one is just too good.

You are going to love it — get ready for this one and turn it up!!

Love you so much❤️

Mom
