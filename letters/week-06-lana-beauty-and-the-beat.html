Week 6 — Lana

Beauty and the Beat — The Go-Go’s

This album came out when I was a sophomore in high school. I loved The Go-Go’s, and I loved this record. I still know all the songs.

It’s ranked #400 on the Top 500, which makes sense to me. The first single, Our Lips Are Sealed, was so good.

There really weren’t girl bands like this at the time — at least not in my world. They didn’t look hardened or mean like a lot of women in rock. They felt approachable, fun, and different.

This album is just fun to listen to. It’s hard to pick a favorite — it’s more like picking the few I like less: We Got the Beat, Can’t Stop the World, Skidmarks on My Heart, and You Can’t Walk in Your Sleep. The rest are so good I couldn’t even rank them.

This was my first concert. They opened for The Police (I’ve never really been a fan of them). I think The Go-Go’s lip-synced, but it was still a really fun show.

I hope you dance around to this one. 👯‍♀️

— Lana
