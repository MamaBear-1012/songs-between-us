Week 14 – Kayla

My pick for you this week is Colourmeinkindness by Basement.

I have been debating for weeks which album to start you off with and I decided to introduce you the way I found them and what drew me to them. Which was this one, their second album. Basement is dear to my heart.

They were big in the same genre and time period as Front Bottoms, and Modern Baseball and Foxing. What I love about this album in particular is how grunge the sound is. Like it really brings me back to 90’s grunge music. Which reminds me a lot of you and being a little kid. I love his voice.

They are English. So they rarely ever play here and I finally got tickets to see them play in the city with The Front Bottoms and I chickened out last minute cause I was scared the venue would get bombed. lol it was the height of ISIS but something I def regret.

I love the whole album. But my stand outs: Breathe, Spoiled, Pine, Black, Comfort, Wish, Covet. I just named the whole album basically back lol. It’s just so good. I hope you like it.
