Week 37

From: Kayla

Artist: The Front Bottoms

Album: Back on Top

Mom,

My pick this week is Back on Top by The Front Bottoms.

This is the last great album from them. They signed to a bigger record label and put out this album first. You can start to hear their decline in this a little bit. Everything after this album sucks. Brian’s lyrics get corny and it’s so much synth and keyboard.

I included two other songs they released before this record because “Wolfman” is my all-time favorite Front Bottoms song.

I think there’s a great summer vibe to this album. Back on Top came out when we were all in Islamorada together for our family vacation, and it makes me think of that all the time.

This album is a lot about growing up and becoming a better person and outgrowing versions of yourself. This was during the height of me being at UConn and Tamar and I really starting to grow apart. I was still really struggling with Kayla Hay BS but getting to a somewhat less delusional place with it.

My favorites:

  • Motorcycle
  • Summer Shandy
  • Help
  • Laugh Till I Cry
  • Ginger
  • Plastic Flowers

“Cough It Out” and “The Plan” really make me think of Tamar and my friendship at the time and how much we struggled back then.

“West Virginia” and “Wolfman” are some of my favorites. I have very similar experiences to what he is singing about in the songs in such an eerie way. Kayla Hay once said to me she knows why the caged bird sings because of me, and he has a line about it in one of his songs.

“Handcuffs” is a great song about police brutality. I think this was the time of George Floyd and BLM and all that when this came out. I think he tells a great story through song.

“2YL” might be the worst song in the world? I hate it. The lyrics are so bad. This was Ashley and Tamar’s love song, BTW. It’s crazy that this album has some of his best lines and some of the worst things I’ve ever heard.

I’m so curious to hear what you think.

Kayla
