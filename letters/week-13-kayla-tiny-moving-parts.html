Week 13 – Kayla

My pick for you this week is Tiny Moving Parts. They are Midwest emo/ math rock band. Math rock is a little leaf off Rush’s progressive rock.

I love this album. It’s their first one and I drove a lot of pizza around listening to it.

It’s hard to pick favorites because I love them all. Dakota, Vacation Bible School and John P are great.

I really love all the pieces they do with speeches from Brett Favre the QB of the packers at the time.
