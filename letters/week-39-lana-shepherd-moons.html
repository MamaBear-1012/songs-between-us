Week 39

From: Lana

Artist: Enya

Album: Shepherd Moons

Kayla,

This week I send you Shepherd Moons by Enya.

I was settling into our new life and finding my confidence in the choice I made with Dave. I was calming down and finding peace that I had not had for a long time.

I think I heard this at the gym with Lisa and of course she knew who it was - but I just loved it. Enya won Grammys but my musical genre was so narrow - I wouldn’t have heard this.

It is really beautiful. I love all of it. But my top three are Shepherd Moons, How Can I Keep From Singing, and Angeles.

One of my favorite memories with you is driving across the country with you. And we played this at the start of our drive each day with the sun rising before us. You so sweet and little next to me being my copilot. How did I get so lucky to have you - precious you in my life.

I love you with all of my heart.

I wonder if you will remember this.

Mom
