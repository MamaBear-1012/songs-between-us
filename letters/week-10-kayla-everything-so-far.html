Week 10 — Kayla

Album: Everything So Far

My pick for you this week is Pinegrove!!!

I love this band so much. This album really makes me think of summer. I’ve been listening to Pinegrove since my early 20’s. I just found out a few years ago that Kristen Stewart loves them too 🥲 — she even got a tattoo for them.

I love the use of the banjo in this album.

On this album, the songs I love are Problems, Angelina, Size of the Moon, Need 2 (probably their most popular), &, The Metronome, Sunday, On Jet Lag, and Days.

There was a period of time when I played Sunday on every Sunday.

— Kayla
