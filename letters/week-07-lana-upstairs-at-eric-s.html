Week 7 — Lana

Upstairs at Eric’s — Yaz

I hope you really love this one. It was a pivotal album in my little life.

It was a really awkward time for me. I was no longer a competitive gymnast. I tried out for Pom and made it. Twice we did Pom Follies — a get-together of the four high schools in Tempe — and danced to two songs from this album.

We used this album because our captain was really cool and into new wave. Funny enough, it was mostly the outcasts who were into this kind of music back then.

The most popular songs were Don’t Go and Situation. My friend Sivi used to sing the verse as “Goob out — Goob out” (you’ll figure out where 🤣🤣).

This album helped me appreciate my “differentness.” It taught me that it didn’t make me less — it was something to celebrate and be proud of.

Most importantly, it helped me find my people.

The only song that isn’t amazing is I Before E, but I try hard to appreciate it anyway — it’s artistic and cool in its own way.

Monumental album. You will be dancing to this one for sure!!

— Lana
