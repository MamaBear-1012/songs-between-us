Week 40

From: Lana

Artist: Van Morrison

Album: Moondance

Kayla,

This week I have Moondance by Van Morrison.

This came out in 1970, but I didn’t really know anything about him until we went to Otis that first summer in 1997 after we moved to the East Coast, where you met Anthony and the boys.

The other couple that was renting the house with Anthony were playing the CD.

One of my favorite movies as a teenager was An American Werewolf in London, and “Moondance” was in the movie.

And one of Laura’s favorite movies, When a Man Loves a Woman (Meg Ryan plays a mother and wife going to rehab for alcoholism), has the song “Crazy Love.” It breaks my heart every time I hear it. I wish Laura was still here.

“Into the Mystic” was one of the songs for Chloe’s memorial video. Also a heartbreaker. This one hurts to listen to, but I can’t turn it off.

“Caravan,” “Everyone,” “Glad Tidings,” and “Stoned Cold” make me think of the time in Otis and how beautiful everything was becoming, being more stable and turning around.

I hope you like this one.

I love you, always. ❤️

Mom
