Week 50

From: Kayla

Artist: Real Friends

Album: Everyone That Dragged You Here

Mom,

My pick for you this week is Everyone That Dragged You Here by Real Friends.

These guys started it all. I found some of their songs on Tumblr and played them when I was with Tamar on our road trip in the South. She was like, "Dude, these guys are kinda good." So for our birthday, I got us tickets to see them in the city.

They played at this really tiny underground venue. It was maybe 200–300 people. It was an incredible show. Everyone was singing, crowd surfing, and diving off the stage. The lead singer was in the crowd singing with everyone.

This opened our music world to The Front Bottoms, Modern Baseball, Foxing, and Basement. I've probably seen these guys over ten times.

I think the lyrics are a little corny at times, but otherwise I love them.

"Anchor Down," "Floorboards," and "Everything I Never Want to Be" are my fuck you songs.

"Home for Fall" is one of Tamar's and my best friend songs. Back in 2012, she was really wanting to move to San Francisco, and I was begging her to stay.

Kyle, the bassist, is the songwriter for Real Friends. We talked with him at one of the shows and asked him what happened to him and his friend who the song is about. He told us the whole story, and I think he said they didn't talk anymore.

But anytime Tamar used to visit, she would say, "Home for Fall!"

I think you'll like these guys.

Side note: They are from Chicago and very proud of that.

Kayla
