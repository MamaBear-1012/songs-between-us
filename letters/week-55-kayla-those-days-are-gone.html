Week 55

From: Kayla

Artist: Free Throw

Album: Those Days Are Gone

Mom,

My pick for you this week is Those Days Are Gone by Free Throw. Easily in my top five.

I discovered this band through this guy I was friends with on Tumblr. Whenever they come on, I wonder how he is and how his life turned out.

Man, so much I could say about this album. I think this marked a really big shift in how I was thinking of KH. For the better part of my twenties, I was in denial. I was very hopeful, naive, and romanticizing something that wasn't real.

Along the way, I had a few powerful conversations with people who really helped me come to terms with what was going on. Or at least the first layer of it.

By the time I was listening to this album, I was nearing the end of my twenties, and I was really starting to realize how much of what I felt was very one-sided. A lot of the things I had interpreted as some internal struggle were really just shitty behavior.

It was painful. I remember around this time, she texted me in the middle of September to wish me a happy birthday. She confused it with the person she cheated on me with.

And it really hit me. I had spent five or six years anguishing over and believing the best in someone who didn't even know my birthday.

It's interesting to listen back to now because I can remember how angry and hurt I was. But even my own understanding of what was going on has changed so much since then.

I have so little anger about it now. I am so grateful it happened. It's really taught me how to show up for myself and to find my voice. I never thought I would get here.

Also, I think this is exactly how I felt about Tamar too in the last ten years, but I don't really have access to that anger now.

I have been thinking this week about how it feels like these relationships have gone through stages of grief. And I think this album really captures the anger after living in denial and bargaining for so long.

So on that note, a lot of yelling, haha.

This album feels like it was recorded at a house party. I saw them play in this tiny venue in CT where the stage was four inches off the ground, and everyone was screaming their heads off with him.

It was a huge bucket list moment to see them.

I love, love, love the drums in this. I love the guitar too. To me, I think they find the perfect formula for a sound and just do it ten times over for an entire album.

I can't even pick a favorite. I love them all.

But I will say one of my absolute favorites is "Someone Methodically Mushed the Donuts." This whole time I thought it was "Mashed the Potatoes."

Until I was going to write this to you and say how funny it is that I love a song about mashed potatoes. But no, it's donuts. Still a very good song.

I don't know if this is the best album for you while you are working or driving a car, but I hope you enjoy it.

I love you, and thank you for being someone in my life who really loved me and showed up for me.

Kayla
