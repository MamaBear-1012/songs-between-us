Week 54

From: Lana

Artist: Muse

Album: Absolution

Kayla,

For your birthday week, I have Absolution by Muse.

I saw them at the Curiosa Festival. They were the headliner on the second stage.

We walked over to the second stage to see them, and it was a jaw-dropping experience. They had a huge crowd, and Matthew Bellamy is a legendary rock star. It was amazing, honestly. A moment I will never forget.

Like Rush, three guys who are unbelievably talented. Like the B-52's, the way he uses his voice is insane. He is a classically trained pianist, and the way he intertwines that with the music is incredible.

The opening song, "Intro," leads into "Apocalypse Please," and it is sooo good.

My favorites? All of them, honestly.

Beautiful slow songs and headbangers that make me think, how is it possible that just three guys are playing this? Especially on "Stockholm Syndrome."

Popular songs: "Hysteria" and "Time Is Running Out."

I think you will really appreciate how gifted they are and that they are true artists.

I would love to see them live with you.

Turn this up and sing along. It is a fun one!!

I love you!!!!

Mom
