Week 15 – Lana

This week I have Kiss Me, Kiss Me, Kiss Me by The Cure.

I feel like The Head on the Door was where I became a Cure fan. But this album sealed the deal for me.

Lisa and I saw them at Mesa Amphitheater when they were touring this album. It was an outside venue and we stayed in the same hotel as The Cure (until they left). It was summer and it was hot as hell and Robert said he wanted to jump in the moat (a water divide between the stage and the crowd).

I am pretty sure I saw his wife, Mary, walking through the crowd. It was one of the best concerts. When they played Forest the crowd went wild. Now it is expected — but not at that time.

What I love is the shifting mood and brutal honesty.

The first track, when you really listen to the words, is brilliant. It is so rageful but nicely disguised.

Followed by such a sweet romantic song: The Catch.

Then back to rage: Torture.

Then to sweet: If Only Tonight We Could Sleep.

Then silly, but I think a little sarcastic: Why Can’t I Be You (club favorite), followed by How Beautiful You Are — I did a sermon at the UU church one summer on this song. It is one of my favs.

But I think you will see this continually play out as you listen. The constant mood shifting. Dark / brooding to romantic / fantasy to sarcasm / silly.

One More Time, for a really long time, was one of the most beautiful songs I have ever heard. I would tell you when you were younger that this is the song I want played at my funeral.

I think you will love this one. ❤️
