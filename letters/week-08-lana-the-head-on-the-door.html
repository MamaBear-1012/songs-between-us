Week 8 — Lana

The Head on the Door — The Cure

This is where it all began for me — my absolute adoration of The Cure and of Robert Smith.

I went on a weekend trip with a few people (I don’t remember exactly who) to San Diego. Someone had this album on cassette. It was so good.

I remember listening to it nonstop — sitting on the beach with a boombox, then back at the motel sitting outside, smoking cigarettes. I think the people I was with might have wanted to listen to something else, but every chance I got, I put this cassette in and played it over and over.

When I got home, I went straight to the record store and bought it. I played it in my room the same way — over and over. When I went to the car, I brought it with me.

Every song is perfect and beautiful. My original favorites were Six Different Ways and Sinking.

In Between Days and Close to Me were popular on college radio, and they had such cool videos to go with them.

Lisa and I used to do the cutest thing to Close to Me — tapping our fingers to the “ding ding ding” on each other. I still tap my fingers when I hear that song, and I’m filled with happiness thinking about my friendship with Lisa.

The opening line of The Blood — “Who doesn’t love what can never come back” — is about Jesus, but now it makes me think of Chloe, Granny, and Laura.

In concert, they play several songs from this album. A huge super-fan favorite is Push. People go wild, and it just fills me with joy.

The Baby Screams is so Robert Smith. I love his voice, and I love singing that one.

Welcome to your Cure journey ❤️

— Lana
