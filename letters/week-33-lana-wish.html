Kayla,

This week: Wish by The Cure.

This is a great album. Huge variety — classic dark Cure songs, poppy uplifting songs, and some real painful tear jerkers.

I saw them in Phoenix with Dave and we were in the nosebleeds, but it was still amazing.

I almost got to meet them because they were playing at America West Arena and I was a server for the luxury club. My manager said if the band asked for service he would let me do it. But they didn’t schedule till the last minute and there were no cell phones, so I missed out.

They always play “Edge of the Deep Green Sea” in concert. It is one of my favorites — gotta put your hands in the sky!!

Probably #1 is “Doing the Unstuck” and #2 is “End.” Complete polar opposites.

I also like:

  • Open
  • High

“He does cute dance moves to this one — you might remember from the Cure movie.”

Beautiful but heartbreaking and sad:

  • Apart
  • Trust
  • A Letter to Elise
  • To Wish Impossible Things

“This one makes me think of Chloe the most.”

I think you will like this one a lot.

Love you with all my heart. 🥰🥰
