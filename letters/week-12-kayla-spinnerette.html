Week 12 — Kayla

Album: Spinnerette

My pick for you this week is Spinnerette — Brody’s follow-up project to The Distillers.

This album is monolithic to me. It was released the summer I got back from Maryland. I was walking around Borders (remember that store) and saw this sitting on a shelf. The Distillers had been on a five-year hiatus — maybe longer — and this felt like a gift dropped from heaven.

Brody wrote this post-partum after having her daughter and coming clean off years of abusing drugs. I think it’s her magnum opus. I love her voice.

I love every song except Sex Bomb. I always skip that one.

Geeking, the 4th track, is Tamar and I’s first best-friend song — but whenever it comes on there’s one part where we go “not this part” lol. I’m sure you’ll catch it.

The Walking Dead is my favorite song off the album. I’ve got a lot of these songs tattooed on me, so I’m sure you’ll catch some references.

— Kayla
