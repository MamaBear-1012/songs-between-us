Week 36

From: Kayla

Artist: Kesha

Album: Cannibal

Mom,

My pick for you this week is Cannibal by Kesha. I have to send this album because it was so monumental in my life.

What I love about Kesha is that she is one of the only pop stars who doesn’t objectify herself, she objectifies men and writes songs about how stupid they are. Kesha’s mom was originally a punk musician and Kesha says that heavily influenced her. I don’t think you’ll hear it at all, but fun fact haha. Kesha really makes me think of Kiera. Kesha looks exactly like Kiera. And they have the exact same voice. When she was alive, I would always call her Kesha.

My twenties were so heavy and sad. And so much of my music was very serious and sad. Kesha helped me have fun and laugh and be silly. When I listen to her, I remember all my friends and having fun with them and driving around with Tamar and dancing silly.

I love every single song.

“Sleazy” was my beer pong song. I was friends with this really tall tomboy named Tanya and we would put this song on and dance when we played beer pong.

“The Harold Song” was my breakup song. I didn’t even know it until I was hanging out with my old friend DJ and in her room she covered the walls with lyrics and she wrote part of it on her wall.

“Crazy Beautiful Life” I listened to a lot when I first moved back from Arizona. It just makes me think of trying to build my life back.

“Grow a Pear” is a hilarious song.

I hope you have fun listening to this. I also hope Dad likes it too lmao. I keep laughing imagining him listening to it.
