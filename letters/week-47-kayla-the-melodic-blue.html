Week 47

From: Kayla

Artist: Baby Keem

Album: The Melodic Blue

Mom,

My pick for you this week is The Melodic Blue by Baby Keem.

I’ll be honest, I really didn’t think I would ever send you Baby Keemy. But this album I’ve probably listened to as many times as Once More with Feeling by BtVS, potentially more. I don’t think I could ever get sick of it. It was a huge bucket list item to see him perform this summer. Baby Keem is my favorite rap artist. He beats Lil Wayne by a million miles. There are some pretty raunchy lines, which I’m sorry but also a little sad I won’t get to see your reaction to some of the stuff he says.

This album has two themes: how tough, desirable and successful he is and him processing being a teenager and his mom not being there because she was an addict and his struggle with loving her and hurting so much from it.

I’m going to try to not write a dissertation on why I think this album is genius. I could go on how deeply patriarchy and masculinity shape the way trauma gets processed. You can hear him fighting with himself, going back and forth on letting you in a bit to his story and then immediately backing off of it.

You can hear how masculinity shapes the way he processes it by protecting his ego, focusing on success, money and women. It gives him away to distance himself from vulnerability but also now that he is successful, he has earned the right to start to show glimpses of his humanity. I think it’s all intentional.

There is so much I love about this album: his voice (he changes his inflection, his cadence, does silly voices) his songs somehow can feel wise and absurd at the same time. He co-produced the whole album which for a rap album means he made the beats, and he makes these huge arena-like sounds.

You will get exactly what I mean on the first track off the album, “trademark usa.” That song gives you a great idea of how the rest of the album will go. It’s unpredictable, the beat and vocal delivery constantly change. He is hilarious, and poignant. I would say you need to put it on pretty loud to get the full effect.

Another unskippable album. My least favorite song is “pink panties,” you’re not going to like it either.

My favorites:

  • trademark usa
  • range brothers
  • lost souls
  • cocoa
  • family ties
  • booman
  • first order
  • vent

And then tucked in between those crazy bops are these really sad and honest songs like “scapegoats,” “issues,” “scars,” and “16” that really peel back the layers on Keem’s story. Underneath there is a boy who really loves and misses his Mom and has had to come to terms with being in a family of addicts at a young age.

I hope you enjoy this a little bit.

Kayla
