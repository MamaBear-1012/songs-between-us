Moving Pictures — Rush

Week 5.

Rush — Moving Pictures.

My first high school boyfriend, Jason Monkelien, introduced me to this album. I remember hearing the song Red Barchetta and feeling like it was a musical story I could actually visualize.

It was like reading a book — I could see the scenery, the characters, and the story playing out in my mind.

The most popular songs — Tom Sawyer, Limelight, and Vital Signs — are all great, but Red Barchetta is on my personal list of favorite songs.

There are only three members in the band, and the music that those three people can make together is just ridiculously brilliant.

When they were accepted into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame, Alex Lifeson’s “blah blah” speech remains the best speech I have ever seen by anyone.

I hope you love this one. 🥰

— Lana
