Kayla,

This week I have Chocolate Starfish And The Hot Dog Flavored Water by Limp Bizkit.

Another album driven by a music video — “Rollin’.” This is the time of MTV TRL with Carson Daly.

Btw, did not like the bass player’s spooky contacts.

Parts of this album are weird — maybe cringeworthy. They spend too much time being defensive or boastful, and it actually detracts from how good they are — just my opinion.

The first song, “Intro” — weird. The last song, “Outro” — even weirder, but for a different reason.

I like this album because it is just filled with angst and grr. It’s a great stand-up-for-yourself album.

Here are my favorites.

Hot Dog: Not sure how many times they say fuck, and they call out the most offensive NIN song, but the rest is good.

My Generation: Parts are really good.

Full Nelson: Has my favorite line of all time — “your mouth is writing checks that your ass can’t cash.” 🤣

My Way: Love love love this one. It might be one of the best fuck-you songs ever.

Rollin’: My #1 fav — cool video, great song, super fun to sing to.

Livin’ It Up: Tons of references to pop culture of the time.

The One: Kinda beautiful.

Take a Look Around: Sampling Mission: Impossible. Not sure if it was ever used in the movie, but it should have been.

It’ll Be Okay: A hard one. You will know why.

This one is out there for me — I hope you like it.

I love you!!!!

Mom
