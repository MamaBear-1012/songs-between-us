Week 54

From: Kayla

Artist: Veruca Salt

Album: American Thighs

Mom,

My pick for you this week is American Thighs by Veruca Salt.

This is another core album for me. It's the album I have the earliest memories of and have listened to throughout my entire life. I listen to it every birthday, along with Live Through This, and have since I was 22.

It reminds me so much of first being in Connecticut and sitting in the backseat while you drove us to church. Us doing the "cow" thing in "Seether."

I feel very close to Chloe when I listen to this album. There's something that feels very sisterly about them. "Spiderman" and "Forsynthia" make me think so much of her. Do you remember how she loved the "woah ohh" part?

I love the entire album from start to finish. I love the guitar and drums. I love their vocals and how they blend together so well.

When I first listened to this album, I was a kid, so I interpreted things very differently.

Like the song "Number One Blind." I thought she was saying "take my son away," not "sun," so I thought it was a song about a girl losing her kid until you told me it was about blinds.

But for a while, I had a very elaborate child protective services story that would play out in my head whenever I listened.

"Seether," I thought, was about having a crazy younger sister who wouldn't stop attacking you.

As a teenager and young adult, I understood it all so much differently.

My absolute favorites are:

  • Get Back
  • Hail Me
  • Seether
  • Spiderman
  • Celebrate You
  • Wolf
  • Number One Blind
  • Victrola
  • 25

I love you!!!

Kayla
