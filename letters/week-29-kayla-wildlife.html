Week 29

From: Kayla

Artist: La Dispute

Album: Wildlife

Mom,

My little pick for you this week is Wildlife by La Dispute. This is my favorite album by them.

The first song I ever heard from La Dispute was “A Broken Jar,” and I was like what the fuck is this?

Later that day I was telling Hannah how weird this band was and put it on again. And as we’re listening, I’m in my head like… wait omg I actually like this.

And Hannah’s going, “Yeah this is so weird.”

And then I was hooked on them for good.

So many of the songs on this album are heavy hitters.

The song “I See Everything” was playing the night I shut my car off before we found out Chloe died, and the morning of, it was the middle of that song that played.

It’s a song about his teacher in English class who shared her journal entries about her child dying from cancer.

Also, the ending song, “You and I in Unison,” makes me think of Chloe now too.

“King Park” is true La Dispute fans’ favorite song. It’s based on a true story of a Black kid who was shot in Michigan.

I’ve only seen them live once, and they didn’t play it at the show 😔

All the fans outside the venue were complaining about it afterwards.

Other favorites:

  • St. Paul
  • Edit Your Hometown
  • Safer in the Forest / Love Song to Poor Michigan
  • Most Beautiful Bitter Fruit
  • A Poem
  • Edward Benz, 27 Times (the final verse is insane)
  • A Broken Jar
  • All Bruised Bodies and the Whole Heart Shrinks

The love he has for his state and his city is just so cool.

I really do want to visit Grand Rapids at some point.

I love you!!!

Kayla
