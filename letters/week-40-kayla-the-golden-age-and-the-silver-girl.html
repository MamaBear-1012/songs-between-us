Mom,

My pick for you this week is The Golden Age & The Silver Girl by Tyler Lyle.

I stumbled on this album after hearing “I Will Follow Love All The Way Home” in the background of a TV show. This was probably when I was 22 or 23. I listened to this album to death.

The first song, “Golden Age and Silver Girl,” truly might be in my top 5 or top 10 songs of all time. I could never get sick of it.

Stand outs:

  • The Winemaker’s Love Song
  • Things Are Better
  • Anyhow
  • Love Is Not Enough
  • Find My Way
  • California
  • For Love to Come
  • When I Say That I Love You
  • I Will Follow Love All The Way Home
  • I’ll Sing You a Song

I might have just named every song lol.

I love the use of the banjo in this. I love his voice. I’m so curious what you think. I really don’t know how you will feel about it.
