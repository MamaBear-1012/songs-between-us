This week February Son by Oleander. I love this album. I have listened to it hundreds of times. When I was driving from CT to Mass a few times a week, this was my soundtrack.

I love the lead singer's voice.

The opening song is a great head banger.

My faves:

  • Stupid
  • Down When I Am Loaded (until today thought it was Down When I Am Lonely 🤣)
  • Why I’m Here
  • How Could I

The other songs are good too.

They even do a remake of The Cure’s “Boys Don’t Cry,” which is really good. I don’t particularly like any remakes of The Cure, but this one is good.

I think you might remember this one. Play it loud and do your coolest air guitar moves!!
