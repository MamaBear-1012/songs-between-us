Week 21

From: Lana

Artist: The The

Album: Soul Mining

Kayla,

This week I am choosing Soul Mining by The The.

Someone gave this to me on cassette. This album was on one side, and I’m pretty sure Pornography by The Cure was on the other.

It was one of a handful of cassette tapes that survived my car break-in that life-changing night in Austin, Texas.

The most popular song, which you have probably heard in a movie, is “This Is the Day.”

I love all the songs on this album. They make me think deeply about life.

Each song is unique and has a different kind of sound, but his soothing voice and his aspirational, honest, and vulnerable lyrics are really special.

My favorites are:

  • This Is the Day
  • The Sinking Feeling
  • Uncertain Smile
  • The Twilight
  • Soul Mining
  • Perfect

I pretty much named them all.

I hear a little bit of Frankie in here.

I think you might really like this one. Don’t let the first song throw you off.

I love you.

Mom
