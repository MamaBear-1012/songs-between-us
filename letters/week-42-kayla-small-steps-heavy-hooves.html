Artist: Dear and the Headlights

Album: Small Steps, Heavy Hooves

Mom,

My pick for you this week is Small Steps, Heavy Hooves by Dear and the Headlights.

I heard these guys one night when I was driving home listening to Pandora, which was a music streaming platform that would play music based on artists you like. I’m pretty sure it was The Front Bottoms radio or something like that, but I heard a song from them and was like, huh, I need to look them up, and then promptly forgot what their name was besides something with “Dear” in the band name.

After months of Googling and trial and error, I finally found them!!

I love this guy’s singing style. His voice is an instrument. It’s just so much more than just singing the song. Truly incredible talent.
