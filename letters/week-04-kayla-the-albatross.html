Week 4 — Kayla

The Albatross — Foxing

My pick for you this week is The Albatross by Foxing. This was their first album, and it stands in my top 10 albums of all time. I’ve seen these guys seven or eight times.

The first time I saw them, I had no idea who they were. They opened for Brand New, and they played My Heart Will Go On by Celine Dion over the speakers before they came out. They were touring with this album, and I loved them immediately.

I remember going back to my apartment and listening to the album on Bandcamp for free. I think some of their later albums you’ll like a lot more.

The first song, Bloodhound, I wanted to be my wedding entrance song for the longest time — lol.

The lead singer also plays the trumpet, and they use it so well. They’ve got unbelievable stage presence. I’ll send you a video or pictures from when I’ve seen them. Some of my favorites from this album are Bloodhound, Inuit, The Medic, Rory, Bit by a Dead Bee Pt. 2, and Quietus.

— Kayla
