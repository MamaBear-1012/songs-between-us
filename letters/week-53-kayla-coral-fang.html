Week 53

From: Kayla

Artist: The Distillers

Album: Coral Fang

Mom,

For the month of October, I'm sending you albums that are Kayla's core albums. All of them sit in my top 10 of all time.

I'm starting you with what started it all, which was Coral Fang by The Distillers.

I think I told you that Maddy Sposito introduced me to them, and then I saw the music video for "Drain the Blood." Brody looked so much like Nancy from The Craft.

I called you because you were at the mall buying CDs at FYE, and I begged you to get this one. At the time, this album cover was getting a lot of heat, so they had replaced the cover with animals all over it. But it had a sticker on the CD with a warning about the cover, and you were like, "What the hell am I buying?" But you bought it anyway (thank you!!!!).

I think about that every time I listen.

This album came out right after Brody and Tim Armstrong from Rancid divorced and when she started dating Josh Homme from Queens of the Stone Age. She got a lot of hatred from the punk community and was shunned, so a lot of her songs are about that and about falling in love with Josh.

I love her vocals. I love all her screams in this. I love the lyrics. She's compared a lot to Courtney Love.

This album makes me think so much of the Caudatowa house. I had a little shrine to The Distillers in this corner of my room, with all the lyrics from the album printed out. I would sit listening to my Walkman, read the lyrics, and look up the words I didn't know in a dictionary.

I love every song, but my absolute favorites are:

  • Dismantle Me
  • The Gallow Is God
  • Hall of Mirrors
  • Love Is Paranoid
  • Tonight You're Only Here to Know

"Drain the Blood" was the first song I ever learned to play on guitar.

"Deathsex" is a crazy song. I can't wait to hear your thoughts on it.

I love you! I hope you're ready to rock out 🤘🏻

Kayla
