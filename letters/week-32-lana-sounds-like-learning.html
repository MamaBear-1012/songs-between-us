Week 32

From: Lana

Artist: Barbara Milne

Album: Sounds Like Learning

Kayla,

For this week I have Sounds Like Learning by Barbara Milne.

So you might be thinking this is a little goofy. But honestly this is another favorite and special time for me with you.

Laura gave me this cassette when you were born. She hated it — which to this day still surprises me. I loved listening to this with you.

It was our jam for many many years. I played it in the car and you had a little cassette player with a microphone. Not sure if you remember it, but we played this in the house so much.

You will immediately recognize “I Love You So Much” and “Sweet Dreams.” They are some of my favorite songs of all time.

I loved learning with you, singing with you, and seeing the world through your new eyes.

You are and remain the greatest gift in my life.

I love you forever and ever.

Mom
