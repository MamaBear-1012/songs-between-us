Week 51

From: Kayla

Artist: Modern Baseball

Album: Holy Ghost

Mom,

My pick for you this week is Holy Ghost by Modern Baseball.

This is their last album before they went on an indefinite hiatus. Bren was really struggling with his mental health. I still hope they get back together or Bren makes music again. He's so talented.

The first half of the album is Jake's songs, and he's dealing with the grief of his grandpa dying. There are some really great songs from him. "Holy Ghost" makes me think of Chloe. I love "Wedding Singer" and "Mass." "Everyday" is so good, and the last verse is just so beautiful.

The second half is Bren, who's struggling with severe mental illness, and every song is so good! I just think he doesn't let his foot off the gas on this album.

"Hiding" makes me think of you and is probably tied for my favorite song on the album. I love "Breathing in Stereo," "Coding These Lukens," and "What If."

"Apple Cider," I think they sound so much like The Killers in that one, it's crazy (like "When You Were Young").

"Just Another Face" is also tied for my favorite. It's got a great message and makes me think of you and all the people I love.

I hope you enjoy it. I love you ❤️

Kayla
