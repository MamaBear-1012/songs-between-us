Mom,

This week, I’m sending you Simple Math by Manchester Orchestra.

This is an incredible album.

What I love the most in an overarching way is this album contains some incredible rock songs that just melt your face right off, Andy Hull’s voice (so angelic), and his exploration of self and relationships in his songs is just so moving.

I love the entire atmosphere of this album.

Absolute rock bangers:

  • Mighty
  • Pensacola
  • April Fool
  • Pale Black Eye

(Your face = 🫠)

The song “Virgin” is arguably my all-time favorite song by Manchester.

“Leave It Alone” and “Apprehension” are incredibly moving.

“Leave It Alone” almost always makes me cry. I think of so many different people when I listen to it.

I can’t wait to hear what you think!!!
