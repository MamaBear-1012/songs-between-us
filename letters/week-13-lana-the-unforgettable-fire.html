Week 13 – Lana

This week is The Unforgettable Fire by U2

I am not the hugest U2 fan but they have had a few profound moments that are stand outs in my life.

I think the first music video I ever saw was Gloria by U2. It was amazing.

This album is simply beautiful. I used to love sitting in the back of my truck and watch the Arizona Sky and listen to this at night.

I went with my friend Karen to this concert at Compton Terrace (same place I saw the Go Gos). It was sold out probably 25k people all general admission. We got there hours early so we could be up front and were perfectly placed and Karen had a panic attack. And couldn’t breathe. The crowd was pushing so hard I didn’t need to stand on my feet – but I was still upright. Pretty crazy feeling.

I really didn’t want to leave our place and wished that Karen would just figure shit out but that wasn’t going to happen. So I helped get her to a medical tent. And watched the concert from there.

As if that wasn’t enough. A huge fight broke out in the crowd. And Bono stopped singing and was yelling at the two guys fighting to knock it off and to stop it and go their separate ways. It was like incredibly powerful and moving and human. I remember this moment like it was yesterday.

They then started singing Pride – everytime I hear that song I think of this exact moment.

Stand outs for me: A Sort Of Homecoming, The Unforgettable Fire, Pride, Bad, Indian Summer Sky, Elvis Presley and America, and MLK.

I wonder what you will think.
