Week 28

From: Lana

Artist: The Smiths

Album: Louder Than Bombs

Kayla,

This week is Louder Than Bombs by The Smiths.

For me the entire album is simply a beautiful listen. Morrissey’s voice is like no other — it just flows so gracefully. He is really such a gifted artist.

Every time I listen to The Smiths, I think they might be the most talented and authentic group of musicians, and what a shame it is for them and the world that they couldn’t continue working together.

But at the same time, it is so authentic. Everyone has shit they struggle to figure out, and it can easily derail your life and your relationships with others.

I was listening to this when I was in intense therapy in Texas right before I moved back to AZ. I was really trying to get my shit together and make a plan for my future.

I love this entire album honestly. It helped me a lot.

I think it helped me stay positive and hopeful and really drove home that everyone — no matter who you are or what you have or don’t have — life is hard, and everyone is dealt cards that are unfair and unwanted.

Life is a choice of what you are going to do about it.

I love every song on this album and love to sing along to all of them.

But my super favorites are:

  • William, It Was Really Nothing
  • Heaven Knows I’m Miserable Now
  • Ask
  • Back To The Old House
  • Please, Please, Please Let Me Get What I Want
  • Unlovable
  • Asleep

“Ask” always makes me think of Lisa.

“Asleep” today makes me think of Chloe and Laura. When I was younger, it made me think about how fucking hard life can be — and to dig in.

I am pretty sure you are going to love this one.

❤️❤️

Mom
