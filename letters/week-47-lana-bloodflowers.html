Week 47

From: Lana

Artist: The Cure

Album: Bloodflowers

Kayla,

This week I am sending Bloodflowers by The Cure.

I have had an internal debate with myself when I would send this. I listened to it when it first came out. But I didn’t quite get it or appreciate it.

This album is considered the third installment of the Trilogy which is Pornography, Disintegration and Bloodflowers.

The live concert Trilogy in Berlin — I have the DVD — is one of my favorites.

They played all three albums live in order.

Bloodflowers is an intense and deep exploration of what it is to be a human. To live a life, to evolve and to grow old.

I appreciate it more and more as I age.

Out Of This World: sets the tone of the album. It is beautiful and the piano makes it extra special.

Where The Birds Always Sing: always makes me think of you — you will know why in the first verse — it is the thing that I love so much about you — your innate innocence. ❤️

Maybe Someday: My #1 favorite. I love when they play it live. It is super empowering; when you find the strength to stand up for what you want or believe without apology.

The Last Day of Summer: makes me think of how different and changed I am after Chloe’s death. Her death marked the end of a person I used to be.

There Is No If: a really beautiful song about falling in love. It reminds me of moments that Anthony and I shared when we fell in love.

The Loudest Sound: used to make me think of my Grandma & Grandpa but now I find it more relating to me and Anthony. It is about being in a devoted — committed long term relationship. It is so simple yet so profound.

39: I grow to like and appreciate this one a bit more the older I get.

Bloodflowers: a perfect ending, the culmination of life and what really truly matters.

I hope you love this one — it is a special one in my heart.

I love you always and forever.

Mom.
