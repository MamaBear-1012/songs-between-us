From: Kayla

Artist: The Front Bottoms

Album: Rose

Here’s my pick for you this week. Rose by The Front Bottoms. Rose is one of their grandma’s name and it’s named after her.

I love every song on this. It’s short. Only six little songs. But some of my favorites of all time for tfb.

Lipstick Covered, Be Nice to Me and 12 feet deep always make think of Tamar and being young twenty year olds.

Jim bogart is such a cute love song.

Awkward conversations is probably one of my top 5 of all time tfb songs. It reminds me so much of Kayla hay and how shitty that whole dynamic was. Funny enough, there was a horrible time I went to visit her and it was freezing cold in her room because she had the window open in the middle of winter and she was smoking menthol cigarettes.
