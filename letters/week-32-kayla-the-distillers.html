From: Kayla

Artist: The Distillers

Album: The Distillers

Mom,

My pick for you this week is The Distillers 🖤🤘🏻🎸

We’re going back in time because this album I first started listening to in 7th grade.

I listened to this album through middle and high school, and most of my twenties. I can remember being on the school bus and listening to these songs on headphones on a Walkman, then downloading them and listening to them on my first iPod.

This is their first album, so it’s more of her punk roots. I love her voice. I like all the inflections she does in these albums. All the fuck you’s.

Great songs:

  • Oh Serena
  • World Comes Tumbling Down
  • L.A. Girl
  • Ask the Angels
  • Old Scratch
  • Red Carpet
  • Colossus
  • Gypsy Rose Lee
  • The Blackest Years

“Gypsy Rose Lee” makes me think of Chloe because I would make her listen to this all the time when she would hang out with me or drive around with me, and we would sing it together.

The song “Distilla Truant” inspired my AOL email, but I made it when I was 12, so I misspelled it to “traunt.” Molly Hulnick was the first to notice and would always call me Distilla Traunt.

I can’t wait to hear what you think of it.

Kayla
