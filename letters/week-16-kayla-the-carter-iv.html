From: Kayla

Album: The Carter IV

Lana,

⚠️ Warning: Playa Vibes might rise to new heights this week!

My pick for you is The Carter IV by Lil Wayne. Wayne is such an incredible lyricist. His use of wordplay, metaphor and simile are unmatched. What’s more incredible is he doesn’t write lyrics, he just records and comes up on the spot. His team has to print his lyrics before shows so he can refresh but he says once he’s said it, he never forgets it.

This album came out after he was in prison for a year for having an unregistered handgun on his tour bus. It’s also probably his most mild record when it comes to x rated content.

I listened to this album a lot after a bad breakup. What I like about rap is it’s usually very positive - like I’m awesome, my friends are cool, I am gonna focus on getting money. So it’s always helped me get in a positive mood. When you listen, think of all the “n-words” as your competition/opponents like rush order tees. 🖕🏻🖕🏻🖕🏻

Okay so my fave song off the entire record is President Carter. And it immediately follows after Chloe’s favorite song How to Love. The juxtaposition and the contrast between them is very fitting to Chloe and I. Now I get teary eyed at How to Love which is ridiculous to get teary eyed at Lil Wayne but it’s a beautiful song.

My other faves:

  • Blunt Blowin - Molly’s fave too, always think of her
  • Nightmares at the Bottom
  • Abortion
  • John - makes me think of Tyler because he has skate buddies that played this song all the time so me and Ty always make jokes about it
  • It’s Good - Wayne has such a sick diss on Jay Z in this song 😮‍💨

Some fun Easter eggs; Wayne has a popular line he repeats in song, where he goes Weezy F Baby and the F is for/ain’t for…. And then inputs a word with an F.

The only x rated songs are She Will and So Special. I skip So Special every time because I can’t stand John Legend.

I think Chloe is going to have some good laughs in heaven watching you react to this album.

Kayla
