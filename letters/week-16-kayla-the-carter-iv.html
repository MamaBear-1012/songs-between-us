My pick for you is The Carter IV by Lil Wayne. Wayne is such an incredible lyricist. His use of wordplay, metaphor, and simile are unmatched. What’s even more incredible is that he doesn’t write lyrics. He records everything on the spot. His team actually has to print his lyrics before shows so he can refresh, but he says once he’s said it, he never forgets it.

This album came out after he spent a year in prison for having an unregistered handgun on his tour bus. It’s also probably his most mild record when it comes to X-rated content.

I listened to this album a lot after a bad breakup. One thing I love about rap is that it’s usually very positive. It’s all about confidence, momentum, and focusing on getting money. It has always helped pull me into a better headspace. When you listen, think of all the “n-words” as your competition or opponents, like Rush Order Tees. 🖕🏻🖕🏻🖕🏻

My favorite song on the entire record is President Carter. What’s wild is that it comes immediately after Chloe’s favorite song, How to Love. The juxtaposition between the two feels so fitting for Chloe and me. I actually get teary-eyed during How to Love, which feels ridiculous for a Lil Wayne song, but it’s genuinely beautiful.

Other favorites include Blunt Blowin (also Molly’s favorite, I always think of her), Nightmares at the Bottom, Abortion, John (it makes me think of Tyler because his skate buddies played it nonstop, so Ty and I always joke about it), and It’s Good, which has one of Wayne’s sickest disses toward Jay-Z. 😮‍💨

Some fun Easter eggs. Wayne has a line he repeats throughout his music where he says, “Weezy F Baby and the F is for” or “ain’t for,” followed by a different word each time. The only truly X-rated songs on this album are She Will and So Special. I skip So Special every single time because I cannot stand John Legend.

I think Chloe is going to have some really good laughs in heaven watching you react to this album.
