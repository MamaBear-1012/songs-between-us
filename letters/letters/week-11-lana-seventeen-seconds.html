Week 11 — Lana

Album: Seventeen Seconds

Week 11.

You knew this one was coming.

Seventeen Seconds. This is their second album. It would be hard for me to pick a favorite Cure album, but this one would be in the running for sure.

Several songs are common in their concerts.

#1 of course is A Forest. It is incredible — I think it probably is the hook that a real Cure fan started on — but who knows for sure. It would be interesting.

I didn’t really live in this album so much until the Trilogy Tour in 2011. For Christmas, Dad got us front row tickets. I have always wanted to see Robert’s shoes — not sure why, but it has always been something I wanted to see.

This concert they played their first three albums in order. Super small venue at the Beacon Theater. Up until this point, I would say the later albums like Disintegration or Kiss Me Kiss Me Kiss Me were my favorites. But this tour just pulled me in deeper.

The early albums are more raw than the later albums — so they sound very different.

Play for Today is probably #2 played live. This song is the single reason I want to see The Cure in Europe. The fans hum along and it is simply joyful.

Next most played live are In Your House and M.

Secrets (ssshhh) not as much.

My favorite is probably M, and next would be a toss-up between Seventeen Seconds, A Forest, and Play for Today.

The only one I don’t love is The Final Sound. It is scary and I have to turn the volume down towards the end.

This is The Cure at its finest — the music, lyrics, and Robert Smith’s voice. This is the reason I love them so much.

I think you will fall in love with this one.

— Lana
