Week 49

From: Kayla

Artist: Jason Aldean

Album: My Kinda Party

Mom,

My pick for you this week is My Kinda Party by Jason Aldean.

This was one of the last physical CDs I bought and the first country album I enjoyed. I seriously hated country music until this guy. He was playing a lot on the radio when I lived in Prescott, AZ, which was a very rural part of Arizona, and I really fell in love with the Americana vibe. We would go to dive bars to play pool, and the bars would always have old classics like Hank Williams on. When huge hits would come over the speakers, everyone would be singing so loudly together.

That whole experience really kick-started me not being such a close-minded dick to country music. I bought this album shortly after I moved back and listened to it religiously. I have a little tradition: once it starts to warm up after winter, I drive with the windows down and country music on loud. It just reminds me so much of summer and being outside.
