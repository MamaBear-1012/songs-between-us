Week 53

From: Lana

Artist: The Cure

Album: The Cure

Kayla,

Starting the 41 Caudatowa Drive era.

This week I am choosing The Cure by The Cure. This came out in the summer of 2004.

I asked Anthony to go with me to see The Curiosa Festival in Camden, NJ. I even talked him into dressing goth. He wore his Doc Martens combat boots, black jeans, and a cool T-shirt.

We had great seats. This was the first time seeing them since we left Arizona. When Robert Smith came out, he was so present and really greeted everyone attending in such a personal way. He still does this today, and it is powerful.

I almost started crying. I just remember, in that moment, feeling so grateful to The Cure and how truly blessed and lucky I was to be there and to have their music in my life.

I really love this album a lot. I wish it got more respect because it is so good.

It has kind of a Kiss Me, Kiss Me, Kiss Me vibe.

It has the beautiful love songs, the angry and pissed-off songs, the you-can-do-it songs, the realizing-that-you-don't-really-know-someone songs, strong political songs, and the songs about just realizing who you are.

I bet you will be able to peg them all.

I love all the songs a lot, so it is hard to pick my top favorites without feeling like I am being dismissive of another song that is just as good.

But here goes:

  • Lost
  • Before Three
  • Alt.End
  • Taking Off
  • The End of the World
  • The Promise
  • Labyrinth
  • Us or Them

I hope you like this one. It holds a special place in my heart.

I love you!!

Mom
