Week 51

From: Lana

Artist: Death Cab for Cutie

Album: Plans

Kayla,

This week I am sending you Plans by Death Cab for Cutie.

I haven't listened to this album since Chloe died because it is just too hard. So, warning...

Before I go to the dark side, something positive. Uncle Evan (Laura's husband) loved this band, and I always think of him calling them Death Cab instead of their full name.

It is a beautiful album. Simple, yet elegant.

It came to me at a very dark time when Chloe was at Four Winds.

When everything I thought I was doing, and the way I was going about being a good mom, just turned upside down.

I thought my relationship with Anthony would somehow magically undo the terrible choice I made with Dave. When the caseworker asked me point-blank what I was doing, it was the biggest wake-up call. She told me it was up to me and no one else.

I took that to heart and changed my approach. I was determined to get it right, to show up, and to fight for Chloe. I read whatever I could about bipolar disorder and was scared shitless, but I felt that by sheer will I could change the outcome.

"Marching Bands of Manhattan," "Summer Skin," and "Different Names" all remind me of that time.

"I Will Follow You Into the Dark": When Chloe was really at a low, low point, struggling so much and feeling so alone, this song was playing. I promised her I would always be with her and that I would follow her, just like this song. I am ashamed that I did not keep this promise to her. But I could not leave you, and I could not leave Anthony.

"What Sarah Said": Another hard one. The aftermath of Chloe's death. The world keeps moving ahead, but she's not here.

It's a tough one, but appropriate at this time.

I love you so very, very much, and I am very happy that I am still here and we are doing this.

Thank you for loving me. ❤️

Mom
