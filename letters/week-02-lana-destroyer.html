Week 2 — Lana

Destroyer — KISS

This week: KISS — Destroyer. This came out when I was 10. The band was already legendary at this time, but well outside mainstream music.

I can’t remember if I already had their other albums, but I remember listening over and over to this record. I loved the songwriting and the cleverness of non-musical sounds — I hadn’t heard anything quite like it before.

I remember wanting it in the beginning because of the song Beth, which is so beautiful…. My puppy-love boyfriend, Gary Coker, and his friends played a KISS song for the talent show and he dressed up like Peter Criss. Silly…

KISS is most likely where I began finding music that was outside of mainstream music that was played on the radio, and I thought I was pretty cool for that.

Anyways, I hope you like it.

— Lana
