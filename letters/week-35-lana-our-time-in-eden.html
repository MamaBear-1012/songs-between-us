From: Lana

Artist: 10,000 Maniacs

Album: Our Time in Eden

Kayla,

This week I am choosing Our Time in Eden by 10,000 Maniacs.

I am sure you will remember this one.

Natalie Merchant writes beautiful lyrics, and her voice is simply lovely.

Lots of female empowerment. Lisa loved her. It took me a while to get into this.

I started listening after Chloe was born, during the difficult time with Dave.

There are so many beautiful songs.

“These Are the Days.” “Eden.” “Gold Rush Brides.” And “Circle Dream.”

“Few and Far Between” is kind of dorky music. But I love the message.

“Stockton Gala Days” makes me think of Laura. She was everything I was not.

“Jezebel” makes me think of when I left Dave. I felt so very guilty about the harm and damage it would cause you and Chloe.

“If You Intend” was my empowerment jam after my meeting with Ms. Geri. 💪

“Tolerance” makes me think of the awful times with Dave, how out of control it was getting, and me finally seeing it.

“How You’ve Grown” brings me to tears and makes me think of what a blessing you and Chloe are. I am so very lucky to have you both.

“Noah’s Dove” is heartbreaking listening today. It makes me think of Chloe and Laura and the incredible tragedy and loss.

I think you will love this.

Thank you for being in my life.

I love you so very much.

Mom
