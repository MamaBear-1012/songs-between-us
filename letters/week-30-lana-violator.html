Week 30

From: Lana

Artist: Depeche Mode

Album: Violator

Kayla,

This week is Violator by Depeche Mode.

You are born!!!

This entire album makes me think of you as a baby, and me falling in love with you so deeply.

You had colic, so the nighttime was pretty challenging. We were most often on our own, just the two of us. I was scared and clueless at being a mother and felt helpless when you were crying. But boy, oh boy, my love for you was astonishing and grew more each and every moment.

This was the first or second CD I ever owned.

I can vividly remember how our nights would start — our tiny house, the kitchen where I would bathe you, the living room and the chair I would rock you in, and your room I had so much fun decorating.

The first few songs, “World in My Eyes,” “Personal Jesus,” and “Halo,” were how the night began. You would start to get a little fussy, so I would dance with you cradled to my chest and sing to you. It would seem to calm you for a bit.

Then you would really start to cry, so it was bathtime always to “Waiting for the Night.” Such a beautiful song. I loved listening to that and cleaning you up in your baby bathtub in the kitchen sink, then putting your baby lotion on and dressing you in your little nightgown.

Soon you would have enough of that, so it was back to dancing and me singing. “Enjoy the Silence” was my favorite, especially the line “All I ever wanted, all I ever needed is here in my arms.” It couldn’t have been more true. I was the luckiest girl on the planet to have you.

We would get to “Policy of Truth,” another good one, and sometimes to “Blue Dress,” which was also an homage to how incredible you are.

The rest were a little too devilish, and by that time you were full blown screaming your brains out. We couldn’t hear any music even if it was turned up loud. So that was probably a good thing — those songs are a little too dark.

This album to me is honestly about you and what an incredible gift you were and are to me.

I wish so much that you could remember this special time.

Thank you for being in my life. I really am so lucky that God gave me you. ❤️❤️❤️

Mom
