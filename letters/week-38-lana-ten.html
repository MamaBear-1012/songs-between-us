Kayla,

This week I have Ten from Pearl Jam. I came to this one way after the fact.

A girl that worked for me was super into them, so I gave them a listen. But it wasn’t until after Dave and I split up that I really got into them.

I would listen to this album as loud as I could every other Sunday when I deep cleaned our condo while you and Chloe were with Dad.

It was an interesting time. I was so happy to be out of the relationship with Dave. But the weekends without you and Chloe were brutal. I was so sad I could barely be in our house because it felt empty.

So cleaning to this album playing so loud (I can’t believe the neighbors didn’t call the cops) made it tolerable.

Huge songs on the radio were:

  • Alive
  • Even Flow
  • Jeremy

But my faves are the slow ones that make you think deeply: “Release” and “Garden.” I also love “Black.”

This is one of Lisa’s favorite bands.

Pretty sure you will love this one.

I love you so much. 🥰🥰

Mom
