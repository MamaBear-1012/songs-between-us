Week 34

From: Lana

Artist: The Cranberries

Album: Everybody Else Is Doing It, So Why Can’t We?

Kayla,

This week I have Everybody Else Is Doing It, So Why Can’t We? by The Cranberries.

I think you are going to love this one.

The lead singer Dolores has the most hauntingly beautiful voice.

This album comes at a conflicted time for me similar to The Cure’s Wish from last week.

On one hand the most immense joy in my life was you. On the other hand was a feeling of hopelessness, despair and sadness about my relationship with Dave.

This came out right around the time when I was starting to find my voice (barely a whisper) but it was emerging. I know I sound crazy but this album helped me to build a strong Lana out of ruins and rubble.

Songs played on the radio: “Dreams” and “Linger” are so good.

The opening song “I Still Do” is one that tells you what is about to unfold is going to be special.

My all time favorites:

  • Sunday
  • Pretty
  • I Will Always

“Put Me Down” makes me think of Dave and how I was finding my inner strength.

I can’t wait to hear what you think and which songs resonate for you.

I love you so so much!!!
