From: Kayla

Artist: Bright Eyes

Album: Noise Floor (Rarities: 1998–2005)

Mom,

My pick for you is Noise Floor by Bright Eyes. I had to wait until it was the perfect snow weather for the ambiance of one song.

When I told Tamar I was listening to this and getting ready to send it, she was like “ugh it’s my favorite!!” A lot of these songs I can hear Tamar singing them off key. This whole album was written when Conor Oberst was between the ages of 18-25 years old. Idk how someone is so profound and wise so young.

Favorite tracks: I will be grateful for this day, trees get wheeled away, drunk kid catholic, spent on rainy days, the vanishing act, Weather Reports, seashell tale, devil town, I’ve been eating for you, happy bday to me, and motion sickness.

Weather reports- makes me think of winter in New England. It’s why I waited til this snowstorm.

Happy bday to me - I play this every year on my birthday, I’ve done that for idk 15 years. Every girlfriend I have dated - hates it hahaha but such a great song and damn does it go thru so many different meanings as I have aged.

I have never liked bad blood and or soon you will be leaving your man cause Tamar liked those songs when we were beefing when I dated Kayla Wray so I can’t listen to them because I can’t like unhear her angle so maybe you will appreciate them.
