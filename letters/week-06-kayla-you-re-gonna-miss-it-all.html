Week 6 — Kayla

You’re Gonna Miss It All — Modern Baseball

Here’s your pick for the week — Modern Baseball. Another great Midwest emo band. They were pretty big alongside The Front Bottoms in the 2010s.

I’ve seen them eight times or more. There are two different guys who sing, and they all met in college and formed the band. They went on indefinite hiatus in 2017 😭

I love this album because I think they really capture what it’s like to be a young adult in the 2000s, growing up with cell phones and social media.

Some of my favorite songs on this album are Broken Cash Machine, The Old Gospel Choir, Your Graduation (!!!!), and Two Good Things.

I think you will really like them 🤔

— Kayla
