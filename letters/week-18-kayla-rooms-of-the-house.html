My pick for you this week is Rooms of the House by La Dispute. Another great winter album.

This was the first album I got into by La Dispute. The first song I heard was “Objects in Space.”

What I love the most about this album is how it takes historic tragedies in Michigan and connects them to breakups, death, and generational trauma. I’ve always said these guys were predictors of Trump’s reign. Michigan is a state that really shows the worst impacts of our country, drugs, the automotive industry, and infrastructure failures.

This album is close to my heart, and I really hope you like it.

“Hudsonville” is about a historic tornado that ripped through Michigan. “35” is about a highway that collapsed during rush hour, sending cars into the river.

Favorite songs:
Hudsonville, First Reactions, Woman (Reading), Woman (In Mirror), Objects in Space, Mayor of Splitsville, Scenes from a Highway, Stay Happy There, Extraordinary Dinner Party
