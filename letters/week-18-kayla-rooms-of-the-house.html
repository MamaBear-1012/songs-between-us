Week 18

From: Kayla

Artist: La Dispute

Album: Rooms of the House

Mom,

My pick for you this week is Rooms of the House by La Dispute. Another great winter album.

This was the first album I got into by La Dispute. The first song I heard was Objects in Space.

What I love the most about this album is he takes historic tragedies in Michigan and relates them to break ups and dealing with death and generational trauma. I always said these guys were predictors of trump’s reign. Michigan is a state that I think shows the worst impact of our country with drugs, automotive industry, and infrastructure failures. I really love the album, it’s close to my heart and I hope you like it.

Hudsonville is about a historic tornado that ripped through Michigan, 35 is about a highway that collapsed during rush hour and all these cars fell into the river or something.

  • First Reactions
  • Woman Reading
  • Woman in Mirror
  • Objects in Space
  • Mayor of Splitsville
