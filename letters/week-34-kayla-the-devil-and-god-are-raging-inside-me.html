Week 34

From: Kayla

Artist: Brand New

Album: The Devil and God Are Raging Inside Me

My pick for you this week is The Devil and God Are Raging Inside Me by Brand New.

This album has been with me all of my twenties and thirties. It’s the third in their discography. Your Favorite Weapon was their first and Daisy was their fourth.

I’ve seen Brand New play live idk maybe 3 or 4 times now which was quite a feat at first. When they came to tour the first time, Tamar took a train to NYC to meet this guy to buy tickets and turns out they were fake, so a couple years later they toured again and I got us tickets and I think we drove to Virginia to see them. That was when I first saw Foxing play for the first time!

So for the album itself. It’s really hard to pick favorites because they are all so good.

Love:

  • You Won’t Know
  • Sowing Season
  • Degausser
  • Limosine
  • Not the Sun
  • Luca
  • Archers
  • Handcuffs

“Millstone” is an amazing song and I used to always skip it until I saw them play it live and then I was like holy shit I have been sleeping on this song.

“Jesus Christ” used to make me think of Kayla Hay. But after Chloe died, I think of her now. Tamar and I saw Brand New after Chloe died maybe a month later and when they played this song I started crying so hard. I often have to skip it when it comes on because I’ll cry. But a really good song!

I was so torn on sending you this one or their sophomore album. I’m dying to know what you think.

I love you
