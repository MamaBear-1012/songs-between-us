Week 48

From: Kayla

Artist: The Honorary Title

Album: Anything Else but the Truth

Mom,

My pick for you this week is Anything Else but the Truth by The Honorary Title.

I discovered this band through One Tree Hill (which was another Chloe suggested show). The early years of my friendship with Tamar, we were obsessed with One Tree Hill together. In fact, how we met Jackie was at the check out when she was working at Best Buy and we were buying the latest season to watch at our apartment.

Tamar was really into them at first and so I threw this album in with a huge playlist of Modern Baseball, The Front Bottoms and similar bands that I would deliver pizza to. This album starts to mark a turn in my grief around KH where I was seeing things for what they were. I think I just started to have a little bit more respect for myself and let go of this fantasy and face the truth of who she was. Which I think that’s the whole point of the album title that we will hold on to anything but the truth.

I really like his voice. He’s fun to sing especially the song “Points Underneath” I belt that one randomly all the time. I love the guitars, I think the melodies and riffs keep a lot of the songs interesting.

Favorites:

  • Frame by Frame
  • Bridge and Tunnel (that’s my happy song always puts me in a good mood)
  • Everything I Once Had
  • Anything Else but the Truth
  • Revealing Too Much (belt this last verse a million times in my car)
  • Disengage
  • Petals
  • Soft, Pale and Pure
  • Never Said
  • Reason to Celebrate

And I prefer the alternate version of “The Smoking Pose” to the original. I wonder which one you will like more. I love both versions of “Bridge and Tunnel.”

I can’t wait to hear what you think!!!

Kayla
