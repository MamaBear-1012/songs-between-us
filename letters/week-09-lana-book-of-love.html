Week 9 — Lana

Album: Book of Love

Week 9.

I present Book of Love. I am sure you might remember this.

This is the 80’s on steroids. Super synthy (just made that up). Boy was how I found this band. When they would play this in the club, me and my friends would almost trip over ourselves to get to the dance floor. This would be a gateway to my love of alternative music.

I love how positive and happy this music is — it just makes me smile and feel like singing and dancing around.

My favorites are Boy, Book of Love, Happy Day, I Touch Roses, Yellow Sky, Lost In Your Eyes, You Make Me Feel So Good.

So many movies have used songs from this album — it is just beautiful.

It is an especially great record to run to — it has a great beat — and I can never get pissed off listening to it. I remember doing some really long runs. And if the music had a negative energy it would annoy me a lot.

I hope you are be-bopping around to this one! And I really hope it makes you smile.

I love you. ❤️

— Lana
