Week 52

From: Kayla

Artist: Hole

Album: Live Through This

Mom,

I have loved sharing music with you this past year. I have learned so much about you and feel very lucky to call you my mom. You are a connoisseur of rock music.

Hole is so special to me, and I have you to thank for that. I downloaded both the CDs you had onto my iPod and would listen to them on my bus rides to school or in the backseat with you, back when you moonlighted as a chauffeur.

This is an album I have played every year on my birthday as a tradition since I was 22.

I love every song, so it's hard to pick favorites. "Jennifer's Body" is amazing. I love "She Walks on Me" and "Gutless." The lyrics are so good.

"Asking for It," "Doll Parts," and "I Think That I Would Die" are incredible and raw.

"Rock Star" always makes me think of Molly because we loved this song in high school. It's Courtney Love making fun of Bikini Kill and the riot grrrl movement. It's so funny.

Now for Celebrity Skin.

I love this album, and it enrages me when people say she killed him, because if anyone listened to this album, I don't think they would walk away and say anything like that.

Again, unskippable. I have always loved "Northern Star" and "Reasons to Be Beautiful." "Malibu" and "Dying" are gut-wrenching.

"Playing Your Song" is so good, and I read somewhere that it's her playing the "Smells Like Teen Spirit" riff backwards.

"Petals" and "Heaven Tonight" are amazing too.

I am so excited we get to share this together.

Kayla
