From: Lana

Kayla,

This week I am sending Morning View from Incubus.

I feel bad about this one because I am not sure how you might feel. I remember you telling me that Kayla W’s favorite band was Incubus. I was excited for that because I do really like them. But that is probably the best and most positive memory I have about her, is you telling me that. 😳

I LOVED the music video for “Wish You Were Here.” It might be in my top two! So when I was driving back and forth to Boston, I listened to Howard Stern and he had these guys on. They were really interesting. The guy who plays the turntables was telling the story of how he explained to his dad that he needed money for turntables, not 1 but 2, and maybe they should have picked another name for their band. It was a great interview and definitely got me interested. Plus, I loved the video, so I got the album.

It is kind of all over the place musically. There is literally something for everyone.

My top favs:

  • Morning View
  • Nice To Know You
  • Wish You Were Here
  • Just a Phase - really beautiful, the guitar, his voice, and the lyrics.
  • Echo
  • Are You In - super fun to sing to.

If for nothing else, they are talented and unique.

I hope you like it.

Love you.

Mom
