Week 11 — Kayla

Album: Mean Everything to Nothing

My pick for you is Mean Everything to Nothing by Manchester Orchestra. This is their sophomore album, and it was my introduction to them — well, this and their first album. I really can’t remember how I discovered them; they must have been suggested by Spotify. But I was driving for Joe’s at the time and listened to the crap out of this record.

I’ve probably said it before, but Manchester is one of those bands that reminds me of the CT weather saying: if you don’t like the melody, wait one minute and it will change. I think you can hear that the most on this album. So many times I’ll be like, ugh, okay, I get it — I want to skip ahead — and then I’m like, oh wait… he does something different in a little. I don’t know if that translates well. I tried 🤷‍♀️

Hard to pick favorites. Shake It Out is so good, but I wasn’t crazy about it until I saw it live and then I got it. You see some 50-year-olds go crazy for them in the crowd — it’s so cool.

In My Teeth, 100 Dollars, Pride, Tony the Tiger, and The River are so good.

I Can Feel a Hot One makes me think of Chloe dying. It makes me think a lot about the day we found out she had passed. So I’m sorry — it’s a heavy one. And Jimmy, He Whispers makes me think of her too.

Just get ready to be rocking out, then crying, and then rocking out again.

— Kayla
