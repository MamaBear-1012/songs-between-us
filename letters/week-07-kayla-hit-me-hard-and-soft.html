Week 7 — Kayla

Hit Me Hard and Soft — Billie Eilish

My pick for you this week is Billie. This album came out two summers ago and I still listen to it all the time. Spotify sends you your listening stats every year, and in 2023 I was in the top 0.5% of listeners for this album 🤣

It’s also a great album to run to.

When I first listened to this, I wasn’t really a Billie fan. I liked a few of her songs, but that was it. The first two times I listened all the way through, I remember thinking, “well… that was pretty mediocre and basic.”

But somewhere between the third and fourth listen, the album completely transformed into one of the most beautiful pieces of music I’ve ever heard.

Billie has something called synesthesia, which means she sees color when she hears music. What colors does this album make you think of?

I don’t know how much you know about Billie, but she and her brother write and record all of the music together. They’ve also pioneered a new way of editing and layering vocal tracks. Sometimes there are 50 different vocal tracks of her voice in a single song.

It’s actually pretty incredible — and I say this as someone who used to be a little snobby about pop music.

I love every song on this album except Lunch, which is the single. I wish they would take the last three songs and turn them into one long music video.

Chihiro is probably my favorite song on the album — I could listen to it on a loop. Blue is not far behind.

I wonder what you will think 🤔

— Kayla
