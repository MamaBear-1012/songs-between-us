From: Lana

Artist: The Cure

Album: Wild Mood Swings

Kayla,

This week I have Wild Mood Swings by The Cure.

When it first came out, I didn’t like it. I think the horns threw me off because I am not a fan of them.

But some of my all-time favorite songs are on this one.

“Want” — as I grow older, I appreciate it even more. It is just so true about life.

“This Is a Lie” — love this one. I bought the piano notes for this and learned how to play it on the piano.

“Jupiter Crash” makes me think of you and Chloe as little girls. It is so sweet and beautiful. It has many times been a username for me.

“Numb” makes me think of Chloe and her struggles with addiction. It’s a hard one.

“Treasure” — so beautiful and makes me think of Chloe, Granny, and Laura. And it makes me think of all that was good.

This one is DIFFERENT, but hang in there. There are some brilliant, beautiful gems that I think you will love.

I love you so much!!

Mom
