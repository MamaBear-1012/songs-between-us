Week 1 — Kayla

Talon of the Hawk — The Front Bottoms

Here you go. I give you Talon of the Hawk by The Front Bottoms. They are from New Jersey.

I’ve seen these guys — I don’t know — 10 times or more in concert. They are so much fun to see. Everyone in the crowd sings at the top of their lungs and jumps up and down the whole time.

Track 4, Twin Size Mattress, always makes me think of Keira back when she started going to all these different treatment places all over the country.

I really like the storytelling style of songwriting, and the drums throughout the album are just top tier.
