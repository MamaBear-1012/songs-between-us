🌿 songs-between-us

A weekly album letter exchange by Lana & Kayla.

A mother–daughter project told through music — one album at a time.

Each week, we pick an album for the other to listen to. Then we write a letter about why it matters, what it reminds us of, or what we hope the other person hears in it.

This site is our shared archive — a little musical time capsule we’re building together.

Kayla & Lana 💛

💭 Why We’re Doing This

Music has always been part of our story — long drives, kitchen dancing, concerts, and quiet moments. This project gives us a way to keep sharing, even across distance and busy lives.

Week by week, album by album, this little project keeps finding its way deeper into our lives.
Another week, another album, another letter — the same two people, the same love of music, the same small ritual we keep choosing together.
