🌿 songs-between-us

A weekly album letter exchange by Lana & Kayla.

A mother–daughter project told through music — one album at a time.

Each week, we pick an album for the other to listen to. Then we write a letter about why it matters, what it reminds us of, or what we hope the other person hears in it.

This site is our shared archive — a little musical time capsule we’re building together.

Kayla & Lana 💛

🌱 Why We’re Doing This

Music has always been part of us - in the car, in the kitchen, in quiet moments that didn’t need words. This isn’t a project we planned so much as one we followed.

Each week, we trade albums and letters. Sometimes they’re joyful, sometimes heavy, sometimes just exactly what the other person needed to hear. Over time, it’s become a way to slow down and really listen - to the music, and to each other.

Week by week, album by album, this little exchange keeps opening new doors. It’s the same two people, the same love of music, and a small ritual we keep choosing - again and again.

✨ Thank You

If you’re here by accident, you’ve wandered into something tender. Please be gentle with it.

If you’re here because you love one of us - or both of us - thank you for being part of this. You’re woven into our story more than you know.

And if you’re one of us:
I love you.
